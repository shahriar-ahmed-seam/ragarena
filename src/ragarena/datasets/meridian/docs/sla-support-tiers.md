# Service Level Agreement and Support Tiers

Effective for all contracts signed or renewed after 1 January 2026.

## Tiers

Three commercial tiers are offered: Standard, Business and Enterprise.

## Uptime commitment

Monthly uptime is measured against the availability of the public API,
excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance.

- Standard: 99.5%
- Business: 99.9%
- Enterprise: 99.95%

## First response targets

Measured from ticket creation to a substantive human reply, not an
auto-acknowledgement.

- Standard: 8 business hours
- Business: 2 hours
- Enterprise: 30 minutes

Business hours for the Standard tier are 09:00 to 18:00 UTC, Monday to Friday.
Business and Enterprise response targets apply 24 hours a day, every day.

## Service credits

If measured monthly uptime falls below the committed level, the customer may
claim a credit against the monthly platform fee:

- Below 99.9% but at or above 99.0%: 10%
- Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%: 25%
- Below 95.0%: 50%

Credits are applied to the next invoice and are the sole remedy for missed
uptime. A claim must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the affected
month; claims after that window are not honoured.

Credits do not apply to the sandbox environment, to beta-labelled endpoints, or
to downtime caused by the customer's own integration exceeding published rate
limits.

## Enterprise extras

The Enterprise tier adds a quarterly business review, a dedicated shared Slack
channel with the account team, a named technical account manager, and
notification of breaking API changes at least 180 days before they ship.

## Escalation path for customers

Tickets escalate on request through support engineer, support lead, then the
Director of Customer Engineering. Enterprise customers may additionally page the
on-call team through their dedicated channel for confirmed production outages.
