# Leave and Working Arrangements

Owner: People Operations. Applies to all permanent employees worldwide.

## Annual leave

Every employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to the public holidays of their country of residence. Up to 5 unused days
carry into the following year and must be taken by 31 March.

Leave requests need 2 weeks notice. A request for more than 5 consecutive working
days needs 4 weeks notice so the team can plan coverage.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is uncapped. Notify your manager as early as you reasonably can. A
medical certificate is requested only for an absence longer than 10 consecutive
working days, and is handled by People Operations rather than your manager.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is 18 weeks at full pay for all new parents regardless of gender or
route to parenthood, and can be taken in up to three blocks within the first two
years. There is no minimum tenure requirement.

## Sabbatical

After 5 years of continuous service an employee is eligible for a 4 week paid
sabbatical, in addition to annual leave. Sabbaticals are approved by the
department head and cannot overlap a quarter-end change freeze.

## Working arrangements

The company is remote-first. There is no core-hours requirement beyond a 3 hour
overlap with your team's declared collaboration window.

Two in-person offsites are held each year, one company-wide and one per
department. Travel and accommodation are covered; attendance is expected but not
mandatory.

## On-call compensation

Engineers on the production rotation receive an on-call allowance for each week
held, paid in the following month. A responder paged outside working hours may
take equivalent time back at their own discretion without approval, and is not
expected to be online the following morning.

## Equipment

Each employee receives a home office budget of $1,200 in their first month and a
$400 refresh every second year. Laptops are replaced every three years or sooner
if the device no longer meets the security posture requirement.
